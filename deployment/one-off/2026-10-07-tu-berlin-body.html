Technical University of Berlin

Technical University of Berlin (TU Berlin), officially Technische Universität Berlin, is a public research university in Berlin known for its strong focus on engineering, technology, natural sciences, planning, economics, management, and interdisciplinary research. With around 32,000 students in summer semester 2026, TU Berlin has a substantial international student community.

For international students, TU Berlin offers a wide range of Bachelor's and Master's programmes, although the language of instruction varies considerably by programme. Students applying with international academic qualifications also need to pay particular attention to the university's VPD requirement through uni-assist, programme-specific eligibility, and language requirements.

For Indian students planning to study in Germany, TU Berlin can be particularly relevant for fields ranging from engineering and computer science to architecture, urban planning, mathematics, management, and interdisciplinary technology studies.

Eligibility

TU Berlin Admission Requirements

Admission requirements depend on the degree programme and the applicant's previous qualification.

🎒

Bachelor's Admission Requirements

For Bachelor's applicants, the main requirements generally include:

  • A recognised higher education entrance qualification
  • Programme-specific academic requirements where applicable
  • Required language proficiency
  • Required application documents
  • VPD from uni-assist for applicants with international educational qualifications

TU Berlin states that German is required for all undergraduate degree programmes. Therefore, Indian students looking for a Bachelor's degree should check the language requirements, particularly carefully, before applying.

The exact admission procedure also depends on whether the programme has restricted admission (NC) or open admission.

📚

Master's Admission Requirements

Applicants to a Master's programme generally need:

  • A completed Bachelor's or equivalent first university degree
  • A relevant academic background
  • Programme-specific subject requirements
  • Required language proficiency
  • Required academic documents
  • VPD where applicable

TU Berlin specifically notes that Master's programmes can have additional subject-specific requirements. These may include particular coursework, internships, or other conditions defined by the individual programme.

For Indian students

What Makes the TU Berlin Application Different for Indian Students?

Indian students applying with international academic qualifications need to pay attention to a few additional steps.

📄

VPD through uni-assist

One of the most important requirements is the Preliminary Review Documentation (VPD).

TU Berlin states that applicants with an international university entrance qualification or primary university degree need a VPD from uni-assist. After receiving the VPD, the student must apply through the TU Berlin application portal within the relevant deadline.

The university recommends requesting the VPD early because processing can take 4–6 weeks. The VPD issued for TU Berlin is valid for one year.

🗂️

Academic document evaluation

For Master's applicants with a foreign Bachelor's degree, the application may require:

  • Bachelor's degree certificate
  • Transcript of records
  • Details of subjects and credits
  • Official translations where documents are not issued in German or English
  • VPD from uni-assist
  • Additional programme-specific documents where applicable

TU Berlin's application checklist specifies these academic documents for applicants with a primary university degree from a foreign university.

🗣️

Language certificates

Do not assume that one language requirement applies across all TU Berlin courses.

English-language Master's programmes require proof of English proficiency, while German-language programmes require the applicable German-language qualification. Some programmes may have additional language requirements.

📋

Programme-specific requirements

TU Berlin does not use one universal set of Master's admission requirements for every course. The relevant programme's admission regulations should therefore be checked before preparing the application.

Step by step

TU Berlin Application Process for International Students

The application process can be broadly understood as follows:

  1. Choose your degree programme

    Start by checking the TU Berlin programme database for:

    • Degree level
    • Language of instruction
    • Admission status
    • Programme start
    • Subject-specific requirements

  2. Check your academic eligibility

    Review the admission requirements for your specific Bachelor's or Master's programme.

  3. Apply for the VPD

    If you have an international university entrance qualification or primary university degree, request the TU Berlin VPD through uni-assist.

  4. Prepare your documents

    Depending on the programme, these may include:

    • Academic certificates
    • Transcripts
    • VPD
    • Language certificate
    • CV
    • Motivation letter
    • Programme-specific documents

    Not every document is required for every programme, so applicants should follow the individual course requirements.

  5. Submit the TU Berlin application

    After receiving the required VPD, apply through the relevant TU Berlin application portal before the programme deadline.

  6. Wait for the admission decision

    If admitted, follow the instructions in the admission letter and complete enrolment within the specified deadline.

Student Life at TU Berlin

Studying at TU Berlin provides access to a large university environment in the centre of Berlin.

With more than 32,000 students and nearly 10,000 foreign nationals, the university has a sizeable international community.

Students can also benefit from Berlin's wider academic and research ecosystem. TU Berlin is part of the Berlin University Alliance, linking it with Freie Universität Berlin, Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin, and Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin.

The university also offers academic advising and international information sessions for prospective students.

Technical University of Berlin for Indian Students

For Indian students, TU Berlin can be particularly relevant when the intended field matches its strengths in technology, engineering, natural sciences, planning, and interdisciplinary research. However, the application should be approached programme by programme.

Before applying, Indian applicants should verify:

  • Whether the chosen programme is taught in English or German.
  • Whether the programme is open or admission-restricted.
  • Whether a VPD from uni-assist is required.
  • Whether their Indian qualification meets the academic entry requirements.
  • Which language certificate is accepted.
  • Whether the Master's programme has subject-specific requirements.
  • The exact application deadline.
  • The semester contribution and expected living costs.
  • Any additional documents required by the individual programme.

The VPD point is particularly important because TU Berlin explicitly requires applicants with international university entrance qualifications or primary university degrees to obtain TU Berlin-specific preliminary review documentation through uni-assist.

Verdict

Is TU Berlin Good for International Students?

TU Berlin can be a strong choice for international students looking for a research-oriented public university with a broad technical and interdisciplinary academic portfolio.

Its current student population includes thousands of foreign nationals, while its participation in the Berlin University Alliance and Germany's Excellence Strategy adds a substantial research dimension.

The main consideration is programme fit. Students should not choose TU Berlin only because it is a well-known technical university. The language of instruction, academic prerequisites, admission status, and individual programme structure should all be checked before applying.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About TU Berlin

Is TU Berlin a public university?

Yes. Technische Universität Berlin is a public research university in Berlin, Germany.

Is German required to study at TU Berlin?

It depends on the programme. TU Berlin states that German is required for undergraduate programmes, while Master's programmes can be taught in German, English, or both depending on the course.

What is the TU Berlin application deadline?

There is no single deadline for every programme. Deadlines vary according to degree level, admission status, programme and semester. Applicants should check the specific programme before applying.

What should Indian students check before applying to TU Berlin?

They should check the programme's language, academic eligibility, VPD requirement, programme-specific admission conditions, required documents, and exact application deadline before submitting an application.

Summary
