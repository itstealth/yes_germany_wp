Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich (LMU): Courses, Fees & Admissions for Indian Students

Everything Indian students need to apply to LMU Munich: Bachelor's, Master's and State Examination programmes across 18 faculties, English-taught Master's, a low semester fee, APS guidance, scholarships, and the right application route for your programme.

Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München (LMU) is a public research university and a strong, research-intensive comprehensive university with broad subject coverage. Founded in 1472 as the University of Ingolstadt, it moved to Munich in 1826. LMU Munich is one of Germany's largest universities and is the largest university in Bavaria, with 52,658 students in winter semester 2025/26, around 22% of them international.

Indian applicants must meet LMU's general and programme-specific admission requirements. Depending on the programme, applicants may need an APS certificate, language certification and additional selection or aptitude requirements. LMU does not have one universal application procedure for all programmes.

After successfully completing their degree in Germany, eligible international graduates can obtain a residence permit for up to 18 months to look for qualified employment.

Requirements

LMU Admission Requirements for International Students

There is no single set of requirements applicable to every LMU programme. Requirements differ between Bachelor's and Master's, and programme-specific requirements matter.

🎓

Academic qualification

  • Bachelor's: a university admission qualification. Depending on the applicant's qualification, this may be a school-leaving certificate plus a university entrance examination certificate, or evidence of a year of higher-education study or a university certificate.
  • Master's: a recognised Bachelor's degree with relevant academic content
  • Programme-specific prerequisites and credits
🗣️

Language

  • Most Bachelor's programmes are German-taught and require German-language proof; the exact level and accepted certificate depend on the programme and admission route.
  • LMU's general information describes B2 as the minimum evidence at application in certain unrestricted-admission cases, followed by the required higher-level certificate for registration.
  • English-taught Master's, doctoral and double-degree programmes taught in English are the exception to the German requirement.
  • There is no university-wide IELTS/TOEFL score; English requirements are programme-specific.
📝

Aptitude, selection and tests

  • Some subjects have an aptitude assessment, pre-registration or study-orientation process, for which you must apply additionally, in time, via the respective institute.
  • Some programmes may require or recommend the GRE.
  • LMU has no university-wide GATE or GMAT requirement; some programmes use GMAT (e.g. Business Education Master's), while the regular M.Sc. Economics states GRE/GMAT are not required.
🗂️

Documents for International Office applications

  • Application form
  • University admission qualification, as an officially certified copy in the original language, with a certified translation (certificates in English and several other languages need no translation)
  • APS certificate for school-leaving certificates or degrees from India
  • Evidence of German proficiency (not for English-taught Master's, doctoral and double-degree programmes)
  • Updated CV
  • If you have studied before: all university certificates, diplomas and transcripts

Expert reports, letters of recommendation and internship certificates are not required when applying to the International Office; individual programmes may ask for a motivation letter, CV, references or other documents.

🎯

Numerus clausus (NC)

Popular programmes at LMU can be subject to admission restrictions, and LMU distinguishes national and local NC procedures.

APS verifies Indian educational documents. It should not be understood as the general mechanism by which LMU performs the final NC ranking.

Application

How to Apply to LMU from India

LMU explicitly says there is no single application procedure for all international students. Applicants without German nationality generally apply to the International Office; in certain cases they apply to the Office of the University Registrar (Studentenkanzlei) or the Foundation for University Admission (Stiftung für Hochschulzulassung), and a defined list of Master's programmes uses the online portal MoveIN.

  1. Choose your programme

    Start on the official LMU programme page and confirm degree level, language of instruction, intake, admission procedure and requirements.

  2. Check programme-specific eligibility

    Compare your qualification with the programme's academic, language, and selection or aptitude requirements.

  3. Check whether APS is required

    LMU requires the APS certificate for applicants with school-leaving certificates or university degrees from India.

  4. Complete the programme's specified application procedure

    Prepare the documents your programme lists. Complete programme-specific preliminary documentation only if it is explicitly required. For aptitude-assessment subjects, also register with the institute in time.

  5. Submit to the responsible LMU office, faculty or admission platform

    The International Office, the Studentenkanzlei, the Foundation for University Admission, or MoveIN, depending on your programme.

  6. Complete any aptitude or selection procedure

    Some programmes add tests, interviews or other selection steps.

  7. Receive admission

    Follow the instructions in your admission notice.

  8. Complete registration / enrolment

    Register and pay the semester contribution within the deadline your admission notice gives.

MoveIN Master's programmes: Allgemeine und vergleichende Literaturwissenschaft, Art History, Biochemistry, Bioinformatics, Buchwissenschaft, Chemistry, Epidemiology (winter semester), Germanistische Literaturwissenschaft, History, Informatics, Mathematics, Media Informatics, Mensch-Computer-Interaktion, Quantitative Economics (winter semester), Slavistik, Sociology (winter semester) and Statistics & Data Science.

Fees

LMU Tuition & Semester Fees for International Students

LMU currently does not generally charge tuition fees for its regular degree programmes, but students must pay the semester contribution. LMU programme pages explicitly state no tuition fees while listing the semester contribution; check your programme page for any programme-specific charges.

🎓

Tuition for regular degree programmes

€0

LMU generally does not charge tuition fees for its regular degree programmes.

🧾

Semester contribution, summer semester 2026

€97

The basic fee for the Munich Student Union, paid at registration and every continuation of registration, including a leave-of-absence semester. This is the latest amount LMU lists; check the current semester's fee before you enrol.

No transport ticket included: the additional semester-ticket fee has been discontinued until further notice (since winter semester 2023/24), so only the basic fee is charged. Your LMU student card is not a valid MVV ticket; budget for Munich public transport separately.

Funding

Scholarships and Financial Support at LMU

Scholarship eligibility should always be assessed separately from university admission eligibility.

🏅

Deutschlandstipendium at LMU

€300 / month

For one year, subject to LMU's selection and scholarship rules. Candidates need excellent academic performance and above-average commitment; open to students of all nationalities.

🎓

SIST LMU Study Scholarship

Funded by the Free State of Bavaria for international students and doctoral students registered in regular full-time programmes at LMU. Requirements include academic excellence, demonstrable knowledge in the field and financial need. Apply to the International Office (2026 window: 15 January – 8 March).

📘

SIST LMU Study Completion Scholarship

A semester-long, DAAD-funded scholarship for registered international students with a history of academic excellence who will earn their first degree at LMU within one year and can show financial need.

🤝

SIST LMU Equality Scholarship

A semester-long, DAAD-funded scholarship for registered international students with a history of academic excellence and evidence of financial need who can provide proof of impairment, for example disability, chronic illness and/or a refugee background.

🆘

LMU emergency assistance

International students and doctoral candidates in short-term, unexpected financial need can apply for one-time assistance (LMU Nothilfe).

🔍

DAAD and other funding

Indian students can also explore DAAD scholarship opportunities through the DAAD scholarship database; eligibility, deadlines and eligible programmes vary by scholarship. Erasmus+ supports exchange and double-degree students, and foundations offer further support.

Living costs

Cost of Living and Housing in Munich

Munich is an expensive student city. Actual monthly costs depend heavily on rent, health insurance, food, transport and lifestyle. Students should prepare a realistic budget using current LMU and Munich Student Union guidance.

🧾

Typical monthly expenses include

  • Accommodation
  • Food
  • Health insurance
  • Transportation (the semester fee no longer includes a transport ticket)
  • Phone and internet
  • Study materials
  • Personal expenses
🏠

Rent in Munich (LMU housing information)

Rooms in student housing€400–€600 / month
Rooms in shared apartments and single apartments€600–€1,100 / month
Rooms on the private market (average)About €750 / month

Advertised prices are usually basic rent (Kaltmiete) without utilities; expect a deposit (Kaution) of one to three months' basic rent.

🏢

University housing

LMU students can apply for university housing, much of which is managed by the Munich Student Union (Studierendenwerk München Oberbayern). Places are limited: only about 13% of Munich's roughly 100,000 students live in university housing, mostly in furnished single rooms with a shared kitchen.

📅

Start early, stay safe

  • Finding housing is hardest in September, October and November, at the start of the winter semester.
  • Options: student residences, shared apartments (WG), private rooms and apartments, and temporary accommodation on arrival.
  • Beware of fraud: never transfer deposits or rent before you are sure the room exists, and never email copies of your ID.
  • Admission to LMU does not automatically provide accommodation.
Locations

LMU Campuses and Study Locations

LMU has 125 buildings in and around Munich, and its campus development programme is bringing previously scattered faculties together on five large campus sites. The exact location depends on your programme, so check the location listed on your programme page.

🏛️

Campus Geschwister-Scholl-Platz

The heart of LMU in central Munich and home of the historic Main Building, with facilities for the humanities, culture, law, economics, business, the social sciences and, for the time being, physics.

🌳

Campus English Garden

Where the currently dispersed Faculty of Physics will be brought together in a state-of-the-art campus over the coming years.

🏥

Campus Sendlinger Tor

Home to several clinics and facilities of the Faculty of Medicine; LMU plans a new Building of Geosciences and to bring the Faculty of Mathematics, Informatics and Statistics together here.

🧬

Campus Grosshadern / Martinsried

A natural and life sciences cluster in the southwest of Munich, closely networked with leading non-university research institutions and LMU University Hospital, the second-largest university hospital in Germany.

🐾

Campus Oberschleissheim

About 15 km from the city centre, where almost the entire Faculty of Veterinary Medicine is being settled, creating one of Europe's largest research and training centres for veterinary medicine.

🔭

Beyond Munich

LMU's facilities also include libraries, hospital complexes, agricultural facilities and the Wendelstein Observatory in the Bavarian Alps.

For Indian students

What Makes the LMU Application Different for Indian Students

Indian applicants must meet LMU's general and programme-specific admission requirements. Depending on the programme, applicants may need an APS certificate, language certification and additional selection or aptitude requirements. LMU does not have one universal application procedure for all programmes.

🛂

APS Certificate

LMU requires the certificate from the Akademische Prüfstelle (APS) at the German Embassy for applicants with school-leaving certificates and/or university degrees from India. APS verifies Indian academic documents and educational qualifications.

APS India ↗

📄

Preliminary documentation

LMU does not have a general VPD (uni-assist) requirement for Indian applicants. Complete programme-specific preliminary documentation only if your programme explicitly requires it, and submit your application through the route your programme specifies.

🎒

Documents for Bachelor's applicants

LMU asks for the university entrance qualification, which may consist of a school-leaving certificate plus a university entrance examination certificate or evidence of higher-education study, depending on the applicant's qualification, together with the APS certificate. These are not one universal checklist; follow your programme's requirements.

📚

Documents for Master's applicants

Applicants with Indian university degrees should submit the APS certificate along with university certificates and transcripts. Programme-specific requirements can add a motivation letter, CV, aptitude test, recommendation, specific credits, language proof or a selection interview.

🗣️

Language certification

For English-taught Master's, check your programme's English requirement; LMU has no single IELTS/TOEFL score for all programmes. German-taught programmes, including most Bachelor's, require German-language proof at the level and with the certificate your programme accepts.

🧪

GRE, GMAT and NC

Some Master's programmes may require or recommend the GRE, and some use GMAT; there is no university-wide GATE or GMAT requirement. Popular programmes can be admission-restricted (national or local NC); APS verifies documents and is not the NC ranking mechanism.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions Indian students ask most often about LMU.

Is Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich (LMU) a public university?

Yes. LMU is a public research university in Germany, founded in 1472, with its Main Building in central Munich at Geschwister-Scholl-Platz 1.

Does LMU Munich charge tuition fees?

LMU generally does not charge tuition fees for its regular degree programmes. Students pay a semester contribution (€97 in summer semester 2026), which does not include a public transport ticket. Check your programme page for any programme-specific charges.

Does LMU offer English-taught programmes?

Yes. LMU's current list of English-taught master's programmes includes 38 programmes, such as Statistics and Data Science, Economics, Biochemistry, Astrophysics and Political Science. Most Bachelor's programmes are taught in German.

Is accommodation provided by LMU?

LMU students can apply for university housing, much of which is managed by the Munich Student Union. Places are limited, so students should begin looking for accommodation early; most students in Munich live in private rooms, shared apartments or single apartments.

Can I apply to more than one LMU programme?

Whether you can apply to multiple LMU programmes and how you do so depends on the relevant admission procedure. Check each programme's application rules.

Does LMU offer summer-semester admission?

The LMU International Office recognises both summer and winter application periods (deadlines 15 January and 15 July), while individual programmes determine whether they admit for each semester.

Do I need German if my LMU programme is taught in English?

English-taught Master's programmes generally do not require German for admission, but check your programme's own requirements; some have German-language electives. Most LMU Bachelor's programmes and State Examinations are taught in German and require German-language proof.

Do Indian students need a VPD from uni-assist for LMU?

LMU does not have a general VPD requirement. Indian applicants need the APS certificate; complete programme-specific preliminary documentation only if your programme explicitly requires it.

Can Indian students work while studying at LMU?

Yes, subject to residence rules: generally 140 full days or 280 half days per year, or alternatively up to 20 hours per week under the applicable rules.

Where is LMU located?

LMU's Main Building is at Geschwister-Scholl-Platz 1 in central Munich. Its five campuses are Geschwister-Scholl-Platz, English Garden, Sendlinger Tor, Grosshadern/Martinsried and Oberschleissheim.

Do all LMU Master's programmes have the same requirements?

No. Master's programmes have different academic, language, aptitude assessment and additional requirements, and some are admission-restricted. Always check the programme page.

Does LMU offer scholarships to international students?

Yes. Options include the Deutschlandstipendium (€300 per month for one year), the SIST LMU scholarships for international students, DAAD scholarships and Erasmus+ funding, plus external foundations.
