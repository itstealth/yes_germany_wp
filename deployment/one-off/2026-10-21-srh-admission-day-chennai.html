On the Day

Could You Receive an Admission Decision on the Day?

Eligible applicants may be considered for same-day admission, subject to the university's admission process and the requirements of the selected programme.

The outcome may depend on programme availability, academic eligibility, document verification, and whether all required application steps have been completed.

Please note: Same-day admission is not guaranteed. Some applicants may need to submit additional documents, complete further assessments, or follow up with the admissions team after the event.

A profile evaluation is an opportunity to discuss your qualifications and potential programme suitability. It should not be treated as a formal admission offer or a guarantee of eligibility.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who should attend SRH Admission Day Chennai?
Students planning to pursue higher education in Germany and exploring suitable SRH University programmes.
Can I discuss more than one programme?
Yes. You can discuss your preferred fields of study and ask which programmes may align with your academic background.
Which intake should I apply for?
The suitable intake depends on your chosen programme, eligibility, and application timeline. Check the relevant programme page for current intake details.
Can I attend if I have not finalised my course?
Yes. The event can help you explore relevant study options before deciding which programme to pursue.
Can I ask about application deadlines and intakes?
Yes. You can ask about relevant application timelines, available intakes, and the next steps for your preferred programme.
Will I receive guidance on the application process?
You can discuss the application process and clarify the steps you may need to complete after the event.
How can I prepare for the event?
Review your academic history, shortlist your preferred fields, and prepare questions about eligibility, intakes, and application requirements.
Which documents should I bring?
Carry your available academic transcripts, degree certificates and identification. Check with the event team for the confirmed document checklist and any programme-specific requirements.
Can I ask about English language requirements?
Yes. You can ask about the English language proficiency requirements for your preferred programme and which forms of proof may be accepted.
How can I register for the event?
Complete the registration form on this page once registration is enabled. Further instructions, including the confirmed venue and event timings, will be shared as they become available.
